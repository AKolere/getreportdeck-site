How the pilot works

Built from your exported files. No ad accounts are connected.

You send your exports, we send back a finished weekly PDF report within 2 business days, and your team decides what happens next.

01

You send your files

A campaign-level export for one client account for the last full week (7 days), with the client's time zone and currency. No passwords and no customer names, emails or other personal details.

02

We build your report

Each file is validated and totals are reconciled to any totals you declare. Missing or zero data is shown, not filled in. You get a finished report to review.

03

You review and decide

Your team reviews the report and decides what goes to clients. Campaign actions are recommendations you approve. We never send anything to your clients or change your ad accounts.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is the pilot? +
One agency, one client account and one completed reporting period, at no charge. You send your normal campaign exports from Meta, Google or TikTok. We send back a finished weekly PDF report within 2 business days and walk you through it. You only move to a paid plan if you choose one.
What do you need from us? +
A campaign-level export for one client account for the last full week (7 days) from Meta, Google or TikTok: campaign name, amount spent, impressions, clicks, conversions and, if you track revenue, conversion value (reach is optional). Also the same export for the 4 weeks before, so the report can show what changed. Tell us the client's time zone and the currency (US dollars for the free report). On Growth and Scale, also any targets or monthly budget you want in the report. We don't need passwords or any customer names, emails or other personal details: only the campaign totals.
Which ad platforms are supported? +
The pilot accepts exports for Meta Ads, Google Ads and TikTok Ads. Direct platform connections, GA4 and Shopify are not part of the pilot.
How long does the pilot take? +
We send back the finished report within 2 business days of receiving your files.
Can you send reports directly to clients or Slack? +
No. We send the finished PDF report to your team, and your team decides what goes to clients and when.
What do Growth and Scale add? +
Growth adds creative fatigue and winner analysis, budget pacing from the budget you supply, ROAS contribution and spend-versus-revenue share by channel, target lines and classes when you supply a target, MER, blended CAC and new-customer share when you supply the totals, top-three concentration, and a budget-shift recommendation for your approval. Scale adds your own logo and colours on every report, campaign comparison, a side-by-side channel view, and an owner summary across all your clients: one page each week showing which clients to look at first.
Is anything live today? +
This page is live. The pilot report is built from the files you supply. We don't connect to your ad accounts, and paid billing isn't set up yet.
Do you offer annual discounted pricing? +
Yes. In addition to flexible month-to-month retainers with no long-term lock-in, we offer an annual prepayment plan with a 20% discount. Starter is $1,000/mo ($12,000/yr), Growth is $1,560/mo ($18,720/yr), and Scale starts at $2,600/mo ($31,200/yr). It saves you between $3,000 and $7,800 per year.
What happens to our data? +
Aggregate metrics are kept in the pilot folder for the review period.
